# For Writers

Not producers. Not executives. Writers.

Specifically: writers who are earlier in their process than they want to be. Writers who have a thing they haven't told anyone about yet. Writers who've been working on something for years and aren't sure if that's dedication or delusion. Writers who found a character before they found a story and don't know if that's allowed.

It's allowed.

The file called `the-stupid-basis.md` exists because in 2019 I posted a goofy story to Reddit about a man in a bungalow who wasn't a god. No genre. No architecture. No thirteen rules. Just a character, before anyone including me knew what he was.

That's where 8:12 Theory started.

Everything in this repo — the bible, the camera arc, the thirteen rules, the chime degradation grammar — came after the stupid thing. Not before. The architecture was discovered, not designed. The rules grew because the show needed them: ten became twelve became thirteen, each addition arriving after an episode taught me something I didn't know I needed.

I'm showing you that sequence on purpose.

The repo is organized so you can read it clean (bible first, then Episode 1) or messy (the stupid basis, then the logline, then the rules as they evolved). Read it messy if you're still building. The mess is the truth.

Take whatever's useful. Leave what isn't. The thirteen rules work on any show — not just this one. The camera arc principle works on any visual medium. The `why-i-wrote-it-this-way` documents are me thinking out loud, not prescriptions.

And if you have a stupid basis of your own — a thing that doesn't have a genre yet, a character without a story, a Reddit post nobody read — keep going.

Seven years from now it might have thirteen rules.

Everything I built to get there is in this repo. The scripts. The bible. The rules. The v1 drafts that got called unpitchable. All of it. Open.

Take it.
