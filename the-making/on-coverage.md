# On Coverage

The v1 pitch bible for 8:12 Theory is in this folder. So is the professional coverage it got in March 2026.

Read the coverage. It's a little rude. It was also right.

Here's what took me months to understand: the feedback wasn't about the story. The story was fine. The feedback was about the door. I had built something real and then written around it in a way that only made sense to me — prosey, cryptic, architecturally precise in ways that assumed you already cared. Scott Parisien read it four times and was still partly lost.

He wasn't wrong. I was handing people a building and calling it an invitation.

The mess isn't the problem. The mess is where the thing lives. Every weird sentence in that v1 bible had something true in it. The problem was that I'd made the door the same size as the wall.

So: clean up the door. Not the mess. The door.

---

**On when to pay for coverage:**

Pay for it when you've gone as far as you can go alone and you need someone to tell you where the door is.

Not when you're still finding the story — that's too early, and the notes will send you chasing structural fixes on something that isn't structurally ready to be fixed. Not after you've already gotten it placed — that's too late, it's a vanity exercise.

The right moment is when you think it's done and something still feels off and you can't see it anymore. That's when outside eyes are worth paying for.

The wrong reason to pay for it is to get permission to keep going. That permission is free. You already have it.
